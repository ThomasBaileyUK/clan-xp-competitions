# Clan Competitions

Run skill and boss competitions for your Old School RuneScape clan, straight from the RuneLite sidebar.

- Sign in once with your clan's name and password (ask in your clan chat). You only see your own clan's competitions.
- Pick a competition and press **Join**. Your logged-in character is entered.
- Play as normal. Your XP (skill competitions) or kill count (boss competitions) is sent to the clan server about once a minute while it is changing.
- Watch the live leaderboard in the sidebar or on the clan website. Ended competitions are kept under **Finished events**, split into skilling and bosses.
- After a competition ends, results are checked against the official hiscores.

Boss kills are counted from the game's kill-count messages ("Your Vorkath kill count is: 123"), so they only count while playing on RuneLite with the plugin on.

## Privacy

Online tracking is **off by default**. When you turn it on, the plugin sends your
username and your XP or boss kill count for your clan's competitions to the clan server
(`clan-xp-api.purevibes.workers.dev`). This submits your IP address to a
3rd-party server not controlled or verified by RuneLite developers. Nothing is
sent while it is off.
